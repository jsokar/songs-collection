# 🎶 Song Collection Hub — Classroom Music & Stories Video Library

An interactive, responsive classroom music video hub featuring **173 curated songs and story videos** across **8 collections** and **10 pedagogical categories**. Designed for music teachers, elementary classrooms, and educational performances.

🌐 **Live Website**: [https://jsokar.github.io/songs-collection/](https://jsokar.github.io/songs-collection/)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🎬 173 Curated Songs & Story Videos**: Completely deduplicated master library with titles, pedagogical categories, deck associations, and descriptions.
- **📚 8 Curated Collections**:
  - **Collection 1** (42 videos) — Core Classroom Songs
  - **Collection 2** (75 videos) — Songs & Story Library
  - **Collection 3** (14 videos) — Interactive Music Class
  - **Collection 4** (8 videos) — Winter Songs
  - **Collection 5** (14 videos) — The Littlest Christmas Tree Musical (Vocal & Accompaniment)
  - **Collection 6** (9 videos) — Fun Jazzy & Movement Songs
  - **Collection 7** (10 videos) — The Postman's Adventure Musical
  - **Collection 8** (1 video) — Special Features (*The Forest of Kindness*)
- **🏷️ 10 Pedagogical Categories**:
  1. Welcome & Greetings
  2. Stories & Lessons
  3. Action, Dance & Movement
  4. Animals & Nature
  5. Holidays & Seasons
  6. Expressive & Folk Traditions
  7. Family, Daily Life & Routine
  8. Musical Concepts & Solfège
  9. Friendship & Character
  10. Calming & Lullabies
- **📋 Dual View Modes**:
  - **List View (Default)**: Streamlined, single-line table view with compact numbered collection badges (`1`–`8`), category tags, and one-click play.
  - **Grid View**: Visual cards with category badges, deck tags, descriptions, and quick-action links.
- **🔍 Live Search & Sorting**:
  - Instant client-side search across titles, descriptions, and categories.
  - Sort by Default Order, Title (A–Z), Title (Z–A), Category, or Collection.
- **🎥 Dedicated Video Player Experience**:
  - Scaled 1280px desktop resolution for clean, compact player controls.
  - High-visibility **Accent Green Fullscreen** button (`⛶ Fullscreen` / `✕ Exit Fullscreen`).
  - Next/Previous track navigation with keyboard arrow shortcuts (`←` and `→`).
  - Quick `Back to Songs` button and `Esc` keyboard shortcut.
  - Related videos from the same category for continuous playback.
- **📄 12 Items Per Page**: Compact, clean pagination avoiding long page scrolling.
- **⚡ Zero Build Dependencies**: Standalone single-file HTML/JS using Tailwind CSS via CDN.

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Simply double-click `songs-collection.html` or `index.html` in any modern web browser. No server, node.js, or build steps required.
